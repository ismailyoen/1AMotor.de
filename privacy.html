Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · English version · A German-language version (Datenschutzerklärung) governs for EU/EEA users

This Privacy Policy explains how 1A Motor ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you visit 1amotor.de (the "Site"), an online marketplace for engines and drivetrain components. The Site is operated from Germany. If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), our German Datenschutzerklärung applies to you and takes precedence.

2. Information We Collect

a) Information collected automatically

When you access the Site, our servers and infrastructure providers automatically record technical data such as your IP address, date and time of access, the pages you view, your browser type and operating system, and the referring URL.

b) Information you provide

When you create an account we collect your name, email address, an encrypted password and account type (buyer/dealer). When you post a listing or send an inquiry, we store the information you enter (such as name, email, message and offer details).

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain and secure the Site and your account;
  • To publish your listings and facilitate communication between buyers and sellers;
  • To respond to inquiries and provide customer support;
  • To measure reach and improve our services (only with your consent for optional analytics);
  • To display advertising (only with your consent — see Section 5);
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use strictly necessary cookies to keep you logged in and to operate the Site. Optional cookies for statistics and advertising are only set after you give consent through our cookie banner. You can review or withdraw your choices at any time:

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only with service providers ("processors") who help us operate the Site:

  • Supabase (database, authentication, file storage) — data processed on EU servers;
  • Cloudflare (content delivery and security);
  • Google (advertising, only with your consent).

We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of our users.

7. Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to fulfill the purposes described here and to meet legal retention requirements. After account deletion, personal data is removed within 30 days unless a legal obligation requires longer retention.

8. Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use and disclose;
  • Access and request a copy of your personal information;
  • Delete personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate personal information;
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We do not sell your personal information for monetary value. Because we obtain opt-in consent before setting any advertising cookies, no "sharing" for targeted advertising occurs unless you affirmatively consent — and you can withdraw that consent at any time via Manage cookie settings. We honor recognized opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control (GPC) where applicable. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights. To make a request, email info@1amotor.de.

9. Data Security

All data transfers are encrypted via HTTPS (TLS). Passwords are stored only in hashed form. We apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information, though no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

10. International Data Transfers

The Site is operated from Germany and primarily processes data on servers within the EU. Some processors (e.g., Cloudflare, Google) are based in the United States; such transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated on this page.

13. Contact

For any privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact us at info@1amotor.de.
